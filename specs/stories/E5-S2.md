# E5-S2: Suspend/resume service

**Epic:** E5 — Suspend/Resume & Port-Out Lifecycle
**Layer:** Service
**Group:** E
**Depends on:** E2-S3, E5-S1

## Description

Service using the FSM to suspend an ACTIVE subscription and resume a SUSPENDED one back to ACTIVE, logging each transition (AC-06).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Suspending an ACTIVE subscription transitions it to SUSPENDED and appends a StateTransition record.
2. Resuming a SUSPENDED subscription transitions it back to ACTIVE and appends a StateTransition record.
3. Attempting to suspend a subscription that is not ACTIVE (e.g., already SUSPENDED or PENDING_KYC) raises InvalidSubscriberStateException.
4. Attempting to resume a subscription that is not SUSPENDED raises InvalidSubscriberStateException.
